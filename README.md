## 📰 Where This Market Is Heading

**The constraint is the data, not the model.** Revenue teams are pushing AI agents into the go-to-market stack to route leads, update records, and flag deal risk, and what holds deployments back is not model quality. Data readiness is the primary constraint, since clean, permissioned, unified data has to exist before an agent can act reliably across CRM and enrichment systems. Data integrity has become the frontline bottleneck in RevOps. That is why the least glamorous work I have done, maintaining customer master data and pipeline integrity in Dynamics 365 and SAP, is the part I expect to matter most.

**Building the report is getting commoditized. Framing the question is not.** Natural language interfaces in tools like Gong, Clari, and Salesforce Einstein now let a revenue leader ask a pipeline question in plain English and get an answer in seconds, with no SQL and no ticket to the data team. The assembly step is shrinking. What does not shrink is deciding which question is worth answering and knowing whether the answer holds up.

**Forecast accuracy alone stopped being the goal.** Tariff volatility broke the assumption that historical demand predicts future demand. 72% of trade professionals now name U.S. tariff volatility as the most impactful regulatory change they face, up from 41% the year before. Models built on historical demand curves are increasingly unreliable in that environment, which is why the work moved from producing one number to testing several.

**Inventory strategy is caught between two goals that fight each other.** Companies want less working capital and better service levels at the same time. In 2026 survey data, 54.2% report oversupply problems while 45.8% name low service levels as a critical challenge. Those are largely the same companies, which is why prioritization frameworks beat a single inventory target.

**What this means for the role I am entering.** The analyst market is separating into execution-only work, which is exposed, and strategic work, which is gaining value. The defensible position sits upstream and downstream of the analysis: defining the problem correctly at the front, and getting a decision made at the back.

### 🧠 How I Approach Problems

> Define the problem before you touch the data. A clean answer to the wrong question is still the wrong answer.
>
> An analysis nobody understands is an analysis nobody uses. The last mile is the explanation.
