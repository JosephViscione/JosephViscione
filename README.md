🔭 **I'm currently working on**  
Completing my Master of Science in Business Analytics at Wake Forest University (Class of 2027). Alongside coursework I log play-level NFL and NCAA game data and build statistical models for the PFF Enterprise Database, and I run an independent analytics practice helping small businesses find where revenue is coming from and where cost is leaking out.

🗣️ **How I work**  
I put as much into the explanation as the analysis. Most of my work has gone to people who do not write SQL, including sales leaders, operations managers, and business owners, and the finding only matters if they can act on it. Translating a model into a decision someone actually makes is the part I am best at, and it is why my analysis gets used instead of filed. I also work in Spanish at a professional level.

👯 **I'm looking to collaborate on**  
Pipeline and funnel analysis, quota and comp modeling, channel attribution, retention analysis, demand forecasting, and supply chain and inventory performance. I'm most interested in work where the analysis changes how a team operates, not just how it measures.

🌱 **I'm currently learning**  
Python for analytics and applied statistics through my MSBA coursework, with a focus on forecasting and experimental design.

💬 **Ask me about**  
Figuring out which question is actually worth answering before anyone opens a dataset, CRM data hygiene in Salesforce and HubSpot, commission and quota structure, ABC classification and inventory prioritization, or using Lean Six Sigma to fix a process rather than just measure it.

⚡ **Fun fact**  
I walked on to an NCAA Division I football team in college. Most of what I know about preparation, film study, and repeatable process I learned there first.
